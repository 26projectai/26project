# Sky Coin Obby: launch playbook

No game is guaranteed to go viral. But Roblox's recommendation system rewards a few signals you can
deliberately improve, and those signals are what this game is built around.

Roblox recommends games that people **keep playing**, **come back to**, and **play with friends**.
That makes the first minute, the daily return and friend invites more important than anything else.

## What's already built in, and why

| Signal | Feature in the game |
|---|---|
| **Hook in the first 30 seconds** | Coins straight away, a checkpoint pop-up with +5 coins, and music that kicks in |
| **Small wins every 30–60 seconds** | Coins on every section, coin combos, stage rewards and the "+N" pop-ups |
| **A reason to come back** | Daily streak rewards (25 → 250 coins) and a permanent coin bonus per win |
| **Playing with friends** | +50 coins each for invites, plus the "Invite friends to race you!" button on the win screen |
| **Competition** | Global *Most Wins* and *Fastest Run* boards, and a speedrun timer with your personal best |
| **Novelty worth sharing** | A low-gravity Space world with moon jumps and the jump-pad launch |
| **Progress you don't lose** | Everything saves: stage, coins, wins, cosmetics and your best time |

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Installer run, game plays through all 30 stages in Studio
- [ ] Music and sound IDs pasted into `Assets` (Part B in the README)
- [ ] Icon and thumbnail uploaded (Part C)
- [ ] 2x Coins, VIP and Skip Stage created (Part D)
- [ ] Badges created; free badges are a discovery boost, since players see them on their profile
- [ ] API Services turned on, published, set to **Public**
- [ ] **Title:** `Sky Coin Obby [30 STAGES]`. Once it's live, add an emoji tag like `🚀 SPACE` for updates.
- [ ] **Description** (first line matters most):
      `Jump through 30 stages across 3 worlds! 🌤️ Sky Islands → 🍭 Candy Land → 🚀 Outer Space (LOW GRAVITY!). Collect coins, unlock trails, beat your friends' times and top the global leaderboards!`
- [ ] **Genre:** Obby & Platformer. **Max players:** 20–30, because full servers feel alive.
- [ ] Play on a **phone** too. Most Roblox players are on mobile.

## First 2 weeks

1. **Day 1: soft launch.** Invite 5–10 friends and watch them play. Wherever people die repeatedly,
   make that stage a little easier, because early quitting hurts recommendations the most.
2. **Check Creator Analytics daily** (Creator Hub → your game → Analytics):
   - **Day 1 retention:** aim for over 10%. If it's low, the start is too hard or too boring.
   - **Average session length:** aim for over 8 minutes.
   - **Where players quit:** reach-rate by stage. Fix the stage before the big drop.
3. **Sponsored ads:** start small with a few hundred Robux a day, using the thumbnail, and only
   *after* retention looks healthy. Ads that bring players into a leaky game waste money.
4. **Update weekly.** Each update is a reason for people to return; change the title tag, e.g.
   `[🆕 NEW WORLD]`. Easy update ideas:
   - A new world of 10 stages (Lava Factory, Ice Kingdom, Haunted Mansion)
   - New trails or auras, and seasonal ones (Halloween, Christmas)
   - Limited-time weekend events (2x coins weekend: temporarily set `WinMultiplierStep` higher)

## Content for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels

Short videos are how obbies blow up. Record with Roblox's built-in recorder or OBS:

- **"Can you beat my time?"** Show the speedrun timer and a clean run, with your time on screen.
- **Moon jump fails and wins.** The Space jump-pad launch and near misses on the spinners.
- **Before and after cosmetics.** Grinding coins, then unlocking the Galaxy Trail.
- **"I made a Roblox game"** devlog series. Show building it; this format consistently performs well.

Use the Higgsfield artwork and your trail effects in the video covers. Higgsfield's video tools can
also turn the thumbnail into a short animated teaser.

## Safety notes

- Rewards are coin-based, with no paid random loot boxes, which keeps the game within Roblox's rules.
- Don't ask players to like or favourite in exchange for rewards. Roblox doesn't allow that.
  Asking politely ("Like the game for more worlds!") is fine.
